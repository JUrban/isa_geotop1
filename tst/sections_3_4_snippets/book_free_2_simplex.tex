Let $I$ be the interior of the polygon $J$ in $\mathbf{R}^{2}$. By Theorem 2.2, $\bar{I}$ is a finite polyhedron $|K|$. If $\sigma^{2} \in K$, and $\sigma^{2} \cap J$ consists of one or two edges of $\sigma^{2}$, then $\sigma^{2}$ is free (in $K$ ). Thus, in Figure 3.1, 1, 3, 4, and 7 are free, but 2, 5, and 6 are not.
