Theorem 7. Let $J$ be a polygon in $\mathbf{R}^{2}$, with interior $I$, and let $U$ be an open set containing $\bar{I}$. Then there is a homeomorphism $h: \mathbf{R}^{2} \leftrightarrow \mathbf{R}^{2}$, such that (1) $h(J)$ is the frontier of a 2 -simplex and (2) $h \mid\left(\mathbf{R}^{2}-U\right)$ is the identity.

Proof. In the proof of Theorem 4, we choose our homeomorphisms so that each of them satisfies (2).
